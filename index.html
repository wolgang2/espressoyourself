Our Story

Velvet Mountain Coffee Co.

Velvet Mountain Coffee Co. was founded right here in Canton, Ohio by Tommy B., who’s spent a lifetime learning what separates a good cup from a great one. Every bag starts with beans sourced to the same standard: pesticide-free, mold-free, organic and fair trade — grown by farmers paid what their work is worth.

Velvet Mountain was created to offer thoughtfully selected coffees that transform ordinary moments into meaningful experiences.
